## 👋 Overview
Welcome to my Tableau portfolio! This repository showcases the interactive dashboards and data storytelling projects I developed during my **Data Technician Bootcamp**. Through hands-on analysis of economic, global health, and entertainment datasets, these projects demonstrate how raw data can be transformed into actionable business and commercial insights—skills directly transferable to analyzing **retail, sales, and customer performance metrics**.

---

## 🛠️ Core Technical Skills & Capabilities

* 📈 **Interactive Dashboard Design:** Combining multiple worksheets into cohesive, executive-ready layouts with clear visual hierarchies.
* 🎛️ **Dynamic Filtering & Slicers:** Implementing single-value dropdowns, multi-select checkboxes, range sliders, and global cross-sheet filters for self-service data exploration.
* 🧮 **Calculated Fields & Table Calculations:** Building custom metrics, percentage-of-total breakdowns, and Top-N dynamic rankings.
* 🎨 **Diverse Chart Mastery:**
  * 📊 **Bar & Horizontal Charts:** Comparative ranking across categories, industries, and regions.
  * 📉 **Line Charts:** Tracking multi-year longitudinal trends and growth trajectories.
  * 🥧 **Pie & Packed Bubble Charts:** Visualizing proportional distributions and percentage-change shares.
  * 🗺️ **Geographic Maps & Treemaps:** Mapping regional employment shifts, global health metrics, and hierarchical sector data.
  * 🔵 **Scatter Plots:** Identifying correlations and outliers across multi-variable datasets.
* 📖 **Data Storytelling:** Translating complex datasets into clear narratives that highlight key drivers, market outliers, and strategic takeaways for stakeholders.

## 🚀 Featured Tableau Public Projects

> 💡 **Tip:** Click on any dashboard preview image or button below to launch the live, interactive version on **Tableau Public**.

### 1. 🇬🇧 UK Employment & Industry Growth Analysis (2011–2014)
An economic and commercial sector breakdown examining job growth across British cities and sub-industries—mirroring **regional retail and market-share performance tracking**.

* **🔗 Live Dashboard:** [Explore UK Employment Data on Tableau Public](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/alexander.simpson4273/viz/GBEmploymentData_17900039313330/Dashboard1)
* **📊 Visuals Used:** Hierarchical Treemap, Interactive UK Symbol Map, Packed Bubble Chart, and Ranked Bar Chart.
* **🔍 Key Insights:**
  * Highlighted top-performing commercial sectors, including *Professional, Scientific & Technical* (+134,655) and *Retail Trade*.
  * Enabled city-level slicing across 16 major UK urban hubs to isolate regional percentage shifts.

---

### 2. 🌍 Global Health Insights & Demographic Trends
A multi-dimensional dashboard built using the Gapminder dataset to evaluate global life expectancy, BMI correlations, and healthcare disparities across continents.

* **🔗 Live Dashboard:** [Explore Global Health Insights on Tableau Public](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/alexander.simpson4273/viz/GlobalHealthDashboard_17900837146650/Dashboard1)
* **📊 Visuals Used:** Choropleth World Map, Multi-Series Line Chart, Scatter Plot, Pie Chart (Quick Table Calculation), and Sorted Bar Chart.
* **🔍 Key Insights:**
  * Used a **Top-N filter** to track long-term life expectancy growth in leading nations (Japan, Switzerland, Sweden, Australia, and Iceland).
  * Implemented interactive Country dropdowns, Continent filters, and Year sliders to compare demographic distributions and health metrics.

---

### 3. 🎵 Spotify Genres & Audio Feature Analysis
An exploratory product and consumer preference dashboard analyzing track popularity, tempo, danceability, and musical valence across genres and artists.

* **🔗 Live Dashboard:** [Explore Spotify Track Features on Tableau Public](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/alexander.simpson4273/viz/AnalysisofSpotifyGenresandTrackFeatures/Dashboard1)
* **📊 Visuals Used:** Vertical & Horizontal Bar Charts, Multi-Variable Scatter Plot, and Proportional Pie Chart.
* **🔍 Key Insights:**
  * Discovered a positive correlation between **Average Danceability and Tempo** across mainstream genres, while identifying *Comedy* as a distinct high-danceability, low-tempo outlier.
  * Built an interactive **Artist Name slicer** allowing users to drill down into individual artist catalogs and identify breakout hit tracks versus baseline catalog performance.
